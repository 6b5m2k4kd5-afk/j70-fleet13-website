## Typical Race Day Info

**Skippers' Meeting:** 1 hour before first start  
**Class Rules:** ISAF Rules of Sailing with class modifications  
**Scoring:** Low point scoring (first place = 1 point)  
**Registration:** Due by 2 days before race

### What to Bring

- Valid life jacket for each crew member
- Signed waiver (first time sailors)
- Water and snacks
- Sun protection
