## About Fleet 13

We are a dynamic group of J70 sailors committed to competitive racing, continuous improvement, and building community. Whether you're an experienced racer or new to the sport, Fleet 13 offers coaching, camaraderie, and some of the best racing on Lake Michigan.

### What We Do

- 🏆 **Competitive Racing** - Regular races throughout the season
- 📚 **Coaching & Development** - Skill-building for all levels
- 📸 **Community Events** - Social gatherings and team building
- 🌐 **Regional Participation** - Compete in larger regattas
- 👥 **Network & Share** - Connect with sailors across the J70 class
