# Fleet 13 News & Updates

## Latest News

### May 24, 2024 - Spring Series Heating Up

Congratulations to **Doug Fritz** on the **Quantum** for winning Race 2 last Saturday! The competition was tight with 8 boats competing in challenging conditions.

Standings are very competitive heading into Race 3:
- Quantum - 4 points
- Blue Moon - 4 points (tiebreaker)

- Breeze - 6 points
